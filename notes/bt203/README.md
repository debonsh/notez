# BT203 — Basic Mechanical Engineering

RGPV **BT-203 Basic Mechanical Engineering**, 3L-0T-2P, 4 credits. Paper: 70 marks, three hours,
attempt any five questions of equal marks.

Read `../note.md` before creating or editing anything in this folder — it is the style contract,
and this file is only the index of it.

## Units

| Unit | Subject | Status |
|:--|:--|:--|

## Where the marks are

Unit 1 is 20% of the syllabus. In the December 2020 paper **six of eight questions** fell in
Units 1+2, and three of those were Unit 1 materials questions — Q1a (types of steel), Q1b
(hardness and how it is measured) and Q6a (the iron-carbon diagram). All three are Unit 1 recall
and table work, not calculation.

**If you revise three pages, revise 02, 03 and 08.** They are the highest return per minute in the
subject. The only genuine Unit 1 calculations live in 03 (lever rule), 06 (tensile test) and 07
(Hooke's law).
