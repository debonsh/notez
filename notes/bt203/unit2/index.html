Viva + lab

Unit 2 maps onto the measurement half of the BT-203 practical paper. Expect:

  • Define error, and name the difference between systematic and random error, with one elimination method for each.
  • State the least count and range of the micrometer and the vernier calliper, and why a vernier needs 10 main-scale divisions to equal 9 of its own.
  • Explain why a dial gauge is a comparator rather than an absolute measuring instrument.
  • Give the sine bar relation and one use — measuring a taper, or setting centres for grinding.
  • Name the main parts of a lathe and of a drilling machine, and two operations on each.
  • Name four welding processes and say which one you would choose for a 3 mm steel sheet.
  • Distinguish soldering and brazing from fusion welding by the filler-metal melting point.
Blueprint — how Unit 2 splits

The official syllabus gives Unit 2 a 20% share, and the December 2020 paper put only three short part-questions into it. But that paper was set against the narrow official Unit 2 text. Your MST-1 syllabus page is the wide one, and across the wider 11-paper corpus Unit 2 carries the single most-repeated item in the whole subject (micrometer, 6 appearances) alongside vernier (5), lathe (4), drilling (4), sine bar (4) and temperature measurement (4).

So the practical shape of this unit is: a block of short recall questions on instruments — define it, state the least count, state the range, draw the working — plus two machine questions answered with a parts list and a sketch. Only the micrometer and the sine bar involve a formula. Practise those two numerals and the rest is recall.
