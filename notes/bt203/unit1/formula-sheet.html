Unit 1 Key-Sheet — Materials

Every figure and formula in Unit 1 on one page. Print this and leave the eight lesson files at home.

01 Classification

ferrous (base metal iron)  |  non-ferrous (no iron)
FERROUS -> carbon < 2% = STEEL      (0.03 - 2.0% C)
        -> carbon > 2% = CAST IRON  (2.0 - 6.67% C)
NON-FERROUS -> light (density < 5 g/cm3: Al 2.7, Mg, Ti)
             -> heavy (density > 5 g/cm3: Cu 8.96, Pb, Zn)
             -> precious (Au, Ag, Pt)   -> non-metallic
BOUNDARY TO BOX: 2.0% carbon

02 Cast iron & carbon steels

STEEL bands        CAST IRON types
low    0.03-0.25    white     1.8-3.0%  C as Fe3C, hard + brittle
medium 0.25-0.60    grey      2.5-4.0%  C as graphite FLAKES
high   0.60-1.00    ductile   3.0-4.0%  C as graphite SPHEROIDS
alloy  +Cr/Ni/Mo/V/W malleable 2.5-3.0%  C as ROSETTES
WHY CAST IRON BREAKS: high C -> brittle -> cannot flow plastically
m_e = m x p / 100        add case:  x / (m + x) = p / 100

03 Iron-carbon diagram

eutectoid  0.77% C at 727 degC     eutectic  4.3% C at 1147 degC
steel / cast iron boundary  2.0% C
PHASES (3)      alpha ferrite (soft, max 0.022% C)
                gamma austenite (soft, max 2.0% C)
                cementite Fe3C (hard, brittle, 6.67% C)
MICROCONSTITUENTS (2)  pearlite = alpha + Fe3C lamellae
                      ledeburite = gamma + Fe3C
A3 / Acm / A1(727)  critical lines
LEVER RULE  %phase = (C0 - C_left) / (C_right - C_left) x 100
  below A1  : endpoints 0.022 / 6.67
  pearlite region : endpoints 0.77 / 6.67
  the two answers ALWAYS sum to 100%

04 Alloy steels

Cr  continuous Cr2O3 film;  ABOVE 12% = stainless   cutlery, plant
Ni  stabilises austenite, toughness         cryogenic, corrosion
Mo  hardenability, resists tempering         gears, crankshafts
Mn  sulphur getter, toughness, hardenability rail and wagon steel
V   hard stable carbides, grain refinement   tools, springs, bolts
W   red-hot hardness                         high-speed cutting tools
Si  deoxidiser, graphitiser                  spring steel, cast iron
NAMED GRADES: 18-8 (18 Cr, 8 Ni) | rail 0.5-1.0 C + 1-1.5 Mn
              tool 0.6-1.5 C + Cr/Mo/V/W   | heat-resisting Cr Ni Mo Si

05 Mechanical properties

elasticity   returns to shape          E = stress / strain
plasticity   permanent deformation      %EL, %RA
ductility    drawn to WIRE   (tension)  %EL, %RA
malleability flattened to SHEET (comp)  rolling test
brittleness  fractures, no flow         Izod / Charpy
hardness     resists INDENTATION        Brinell / Rockwell / Vickers
toughness    absorbs energy TO FRACTURE  Izod / Charpy, whole area
resilience   absorbs energy ELASTICALLY area under elastic part
THE FOUR PAIRS: ductility/malleability  hardness/toughness
                strength/hardness       resilience/toughness
A = (pi/4) d^2      sigma = P/A      e = sigma/E

06 Tensile test & stress-strain

SIX STAGES: 1 proportional  2 elastic  3 yield
               4 strain hardening -> UTS  5 necking  6 fracture
E        = slope of the straight-line portion
resilience = area under the elastic portion
toughness  = area under the WHOLE curve
YIELD stress = working limit    UTS = peak, start of necking
ENGINEERING uses ORIGINAL A0 and L0      (valid up to UTS)
TRUE uses the instantaneous area         (rises to fracture)
%EL = (L1 - L0)/L0 x 100
%RA = (A0 - A1)/A0 x 100 = (d0^2 - d1^2)/d0^2 x 100
BRITTLE: steep elastic, NO yield point, NO necking, tiny area

07 Hooke's law & modulus

WITHIN THE ELASTIC LIMIT:
  stress is proportional to strain
  sigma = E . e        e = sigma/E        E = sigma/e
  elongation:  dL = P L0 / (A E)
UNIT CONVERSION:  1 GPa = 1000 MPa = 1000 N/mm^2
  stress in MPa  ->  convert E to N/mm^2 (200 GPa = 200 000)
  strain is DIMENSIONLESS; if it has units, a conversion was skipped
MODULI:  E Young (tension) | K bulk (volume, fluids) | G rigidity (shear)
VALUES: steel 200 GPa | aluminium 70 GPa | lead 15 GPa
STIFFNESS = property of the MATERIAL (E)
FLEXIBILITY = property of the MEMBER (shape, length, section)

08 Hardness & impact testing

BHN = 2P / ( pi . D . (D - d) )     P kgf | D mm | d mm
standard  P = 3000 kgf, D = 10 mm  ->  to ~600 BHN
          P =  500 kgf, D = 10 mm  ->  to ~200 BHN
reverse   d = D - 2P / (pi . D . BHN)
TEST       INDENTER                       LOAD
Brinell    10 mm steel / carbide ball     3000 / 500 kgf
Rockwell   1.5875 mm ball / 120 deg cone  100 / 150 kgf
Vickers    diamond pyramid 136 deg       1 - 30 kgf
Mohs       scratch scale                  -
IZOD   specimen VERTICAL, notch to the blow  -> shear + bending
CHARPY specimen HORIZONTAL, notch underside -> bending only, ~55 J
HARDNESS is SURFACE (indentation).  TOUGHNESS is BULK (impact energy)
HARDENING RAISES HARDNESS AND LOWERS TOUGHNESS
