Blueprint — how Unit 1 splits

BT-203 is 70 marks over three hours, attempt any five questions of equal marks. Unit 1 is 20% of the syllabus, so expect roughly one full 14-mark question and one part-question from Materials in the end-sem, and about 6 of 8 questions in a mid-sem restricted to Units 1+2.

Within Unit 1, the December 2020 paper put three questions on: steel types, hardness measurement, and the iron-carbon diagram. Two of those three are recall and table reproduction, not calculation. The only genuine numerals in Unit 1 come from the tensile test and Hooke's law.

Viva + lab

Unit 1 has no dedicated practical paper, but it is the standard source of viva questions in the lab exam because it is pure recall. Expect:

  • Define hardness and name the three standard tests with the indenter each one uses.
  • State the BHN formula and what each of its four symbols means.
  • What is the difference between ductility and malleability? Give the deformation mode for each.
  • Name the eutectoid and eutectic points of the iron-carbon diagram with their carbon content and temperature.
  • Why does adding chromium to steel make it stainless?
  • Define resilience and toughness and say which curve area each is measured from.
