Viva + lab (BT201 practicals)

Unit 1 maps to Experiment 8: determine Planck's constant with photocell (photoelectric effect → E = hf). Viva loves:

  • State de Broglie hypothesis + write λ = h/p and λ = 1.227/√V nm.
  • What is |ψ|²? State normalization + 4 validity conditions.
  • p̂, Ê, Ĥ — write all three with signs.
  • Why is electron wavelength X-ray order? (microscope principle)
  • Davisson–Germer: what was diffracted, off what, proving what?
  • Full experiment list (15): see official scheme PDF — Newton rings, YDSE, grating λ, Hall effect, PN diode, NA of fiber, laser λ.
Exam blueprint Unit 1 ≈ 8 lectures, 1× 7-mark question guaranteed. Highest ROI: Schrodinger derivation + box energies + one uncertainty proof + one de Broglie numeral. Master those four and Unit 1 is covered.
