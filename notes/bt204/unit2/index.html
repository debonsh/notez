Topics — mid-sem priority order

[01] Surveying fundamentals

» Surveying Fundamentals: Principles, Chain Surveying, Corrections and EDM

Definition and the two principles with reasons; classification on three bases; base line, check line, tie line, offset and ranging; the three chain corrections with their sign rules; and EDM with its advantages over chaining.

Lesson + Cheat-Sheet + 5 MCQs + PYQs + deep numerals + extra-marks tips — done

[02] Levelling and reciprocal levelling

» Levelling and Reciprocal Levelling

Definitions and the instruments; curvature, refraction and collimation errors and why they cancel when the instrument is midway; reciprocal levelling and the two-set-up method across a river or obstacle; local attraction and how it is detected.

queued — reciprocal levelling 4 of 10 papers

[03] Theodolite and total station

» Theodolite and Total Station

The components of a theodolite with a labelled sketch and the purpose of each; temporary and permanent adjustments; the measurement of horizontal and vertical angles; the total station as the modern integration of theodolite and EDM.

queued — theodolite components 3 of 10 papers

[04] Chaining, errors and traversing

» Errors in Chain Surveying and Traversing

Cumulative against compensating errors; errors in chaining and their sources; the included-angle and direct-angle methods of traversing, fore sight and back sight, and the (2n − 4) right-angle check on the included angles of a closed traverse.

queued — traversing 3 of 10 papers

[05] Levelling — the Rise and Fall numerical

» Levelling Book: the Rise and Fall Method and the Arithmetic Check

The levelling book and the two reduction methods; rise and fall computed by comparing consecutive staff readings; reduced levels accumulated; the arithmetic check; and the comparison with the height of collimation method.

Viva + lab

Unit 2 maps onto the surveying half of the BT-204 practical paper. Expect:

  • Name the two principles of surveying and say why each exists.
  • Distinguish a check line from a tie line.
  • State the slope correction formula and its sign.
  • Explain reciprocal levelling and say when it is used rather than ordinary levelling.
  • Name the components of a theodolite, and the purpose of the vertical circle.
  • Reduce a short set of staff readings by the Rise and Fall method and give the arithmetic check.
  • Distinguish the height of collimation method from the Rise and Fall method.
Blueprint — how Unit 2 splits

Unit 2 took 19.1% of the marks asked across ten papers, against 25.7% for Unit 1. But it is where the numericals live: the Rise and Fall levelling book appeared in 4 of the 10 papers with near-identical wording, and it is a full 8-mark question.

So the shape of this unit is: short recall questions on the instruments, plus one calculation — the levelling reduction. Learn the reduction thoroughly and the rest of the unit is definitions.
