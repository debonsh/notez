Topic 01 is done; 02–05 are queued. They are ordered by how often each appears in the ten-paper corpus, not by the syllabus listing. This unit carries the single most-repeated question in BT-204 — the workability of concrete, in 6 of 10 papers — so topic 01 was built first and covers it in full.

Viva + lab

Unit 1 maps onto the materials half of the BT-204 practical paper. Expect:

  • Define workability, and name four factors that affect it.
  • State the nominal size of a brick, and why the nominal size differs from the moulded one.
  • Name the laboratory tests on bricks, and the water absorption limit for a first-class brick.
  • Give the initial and final setting times of ordinary Portland cement, and say what gypsum does.
  • Contrast granite with laterite in three words each.
  • Explain why the water-cement ratio is a compromise.
Blueprint — how Unit 1 splits

Unit 1 is the biggest block in BT-204: it took 25.7% of the marks asked across ten papers, against 19.1% for Unit 2. Almost all of it is recall and property work, with only the quantity numerals needing arithmetic.

So the shape of this unit is: definitions, property tables and test names, plus a handful of short quantity calculations on bricks, cement and water. The workability question alone appeared in 6 of the 10 papers, and it is answered entirely from memory.
